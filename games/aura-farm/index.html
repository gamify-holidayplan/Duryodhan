✨ Aura Farm Defense

The NPCs are coming to drain your aura. Plant your squad and protect the farm.

• Tap a card, then tap a tile to plant it.
• Tap glowing aura orbs to collect them. They're how you pay for plants.
• Aura Blooms farm aura, so plant them early.
• If an NPC reaches your side, the lane's Aura Shield fires once. After that, you lose.
